How to increase website engagement

Website engagement can be increased in multiple ways but one way is by making your website a content rich website

How to start search engine optimisation

To start search engine optimisation, you will need to undertsand your target market.

Three applicable forms of content that can significantly improve SEO performance

1.Video content: Video content can extend a viewer's dwell time which will show search engines that SafariVentures is well liked and will help strengthen its SEO. E.g. Video content includes educational clips, travelers' testimonials, and safari highlight reels which will boost company engagement.

2.Blog articles and destination guides: Well written informative blogs and articles about desinations can provide knowledge and value to potential customers. To improve SEO rankings blogs and articles need to include targeting keywords to drive organic traffic for example “family safari packages Tanzania,” or “best safari in Kenya.”

3.Interactive content: The website can include helpful tools such as safari cost calculators and an itinerary planner to improve user experience and encourage viewers to interact with the site and boost SEO. Enhancing SEO performance through integrating rich media can help boost organic traffic. This will help brands compete with its competitors and reduce bounce rate while making the website more competitive in search engines.
